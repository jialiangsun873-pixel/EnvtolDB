Accuracy & provenance

Every sequence carries its UniProt accession (open it on uniprot.org to compare) and a SHA-256 checksum. Every experimental record carries source database and DOI. Literature anchoring status is recorded per species from a strict Europe PMC co-occurrence search. Human verification flags require written evidence.
