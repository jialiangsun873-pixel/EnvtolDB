Collection protocol per group: species curation criteria, sequence retrieval, literature anchoring, and experimental calibration. Shared pipeline: UniProt taxonomy API exact name resolution → reference proteome (else all proteins of the taxon) → ≥50 aa filter + dedup. Redundancy scope is recorded per pool: thermophilic = 30% cluster representatives; halophilic/pH/solvent = 90% linclust representatives; controls = exact dedup.

Thermophilic

Inclusion criteria — Genus-level OGT tables (Bakelaar et al.; Kernavis et al.) with OGT > 45 °C, plus established hyperthermophile models (Pyrococcus, Methanopyrus, Aquifex, Thermotoga, Sulfolobus). Sequences are 30%-identity cluster representatives (36,297 of a 72,559-protein superset).

Literature anchoring — No literature-anchoring gate (thermal phenotype annotation in UniProt is comprehensive for these well-studied organisms); all 87 species retained.

Experimental calibration — Per-species protein counts and length statistics from the organism-level summary table.

Chinese protocol (原文)

入选标准：文献 genus 级 OGT 表（Bakelaar et al.; Kernavis et al.）筛选 OGT > 45°C，补充 Pyrococcus、Methanopyrus、Aquifex、Thermotoga、Sulfolobus 等超嗜热模式菌。 序列口径：72,559 条聚类超集的 30% 代表（36,297 条），非全量蛋白组。

文献锚定：不设文献锚定门槛（UniProt 对这批模式菌的热表型注释完备），87 种全部保留。

实验校准：蛋白计数与长度统计来自 organism-level 汇总表。

Halophilic

Inclusion criteria — Halobacteria-class haloarchaea plus halophilic bacterial genera with molar-range NaCl optima (Salinibacter, Halomonas, Chromohalobacter, Gracilibacillus, Halobacillus, Oceanobacillus, Marinobacter, Halorhodospira and others); each species graded extreme / moderate by documented NaCl optimum.

Literature anchoring — Europe PMC strict co-occurrence (species × halophilic/halotolerant/salt tolerance/hypersaline in title/abstract): 71/86 species (83%).

Experimental calibration — BacDive 'Halophily' tables scraped for measured NaCl optima in molarity (two-pass crawl); 51 species records. Analyzed subsets: extreme 200,135 / moderate 122,045 sequences.

Chinese protocol (原文)

入选标准：Halobacteria 纲盐古菌 + 文献记录莫尔级 NaCl 最适的耐盐细菌属（Salinibacter、Halomonas、Chromohalobacter、Gracilibacillus、Halobacillus、Oceanobacillus、Marinobacter、Halorhodospira 等），按文献 NaCl 最适分 extreme / moderate 两级。

文献锚定：Europe PMC 严格共现检索（物种名 × halophilic/halotolerant/salt tolerance/hypersaline，标题或摘要）通过 71/86（83%）。

实验校准：BacDive Halophily 表抓取实测 NaCl 最适（% 与 g/L 统一换算 mol/L），两轮抓取后覆盖 51 条物种记录。分析子组：extreme 200,135 / moderate 122,045 序列。

Acid/Alkaliphilic

Inclusion criteria — Acidophiles with optimal growth pH < 3 (16 species: Acidithiobacillus, Ferroplasma, Leptospirillum, Sulfobacillus, Acidiphilium, Picrophilus and related) and alkaliphiles with optimal growth pH > 9 (10 species: Bacillus pseudofirmus OF4, Alkaliphilus oremlandii, Amphibacillus xylanus, Caldalkalibacillus uzonensis, Desulfonatronovibrio and related); kept as separate sub-pools throughout.

Literature anchoring — Co-occurrence search passed for 24/26 species (92%).

Experimental calibration — BacDive optimal-pH values available for 6/16 acidophile species. Analyzed sets: 55,228 acidophilic / 19,899 alkaliphilic sequences (≤2,000 aa).

Chinese protocol (原文)

入选标准：酸嗜极菌 = 最适生长 pH < 3（Acidithiobacillus 三种、Ferroplasma acidiphilum、Leptospirillum ferriphilum、Sulfobacillus、Acidiphilium cryptum、Picrophilus 等，16 种）；碱嗜菌 = 最适 pH > 9（Bacillus pseudofirmus OF4、Alkaliphilus oremlandii、Amphibacillus xylanus、Caldalkalibacillus uzonensis、Desulfonatronovibrio 等，10 种）。两亚池全程分开标记。

文献锚定：共现检索通过 24/26（92%）。

实验校准：BacDive 培养 pH 最适值覆盖 6/16 酸嗜物种。分析集：酸 55,228 / 碱 19,899 序列（≤2000aa）。

Solvent-tolerant

Inclusion criteria — Literature-documented organic-solvent-tolerant organisms: P. putida KT2440/S12/DOT-T1E/mt-2, P. taiwanensis VLB120, Rhodococcus erythropolis/jostii/opacus, A. baylyi ADP1, B. cepacia/vietnamiensis, S. maltophilia K279a, Marinobacter, Alcanivorax. Two routes combined: (P) 15 strain reference proteomes (63,292 sequences, tolerance-adapted background); (A) UniProt full-text 'organic solvent tolerance' search, 14,575 raw hits cleaned to 3,549 protein-level records (e.g. LptD).

Literature anchoring — Graded evidence levels assigned by manual review of strict co-occurrence results plus a second-pass backfill (high 4 / medium 6 / medium-strain-unverified 3 / weak or hydrocarbon-only 3); Comamonas testosteroni excluded from primary analyses.

Experimental calibration — Solvent effects validated experimentally via OrganiZymeDB (see experiments layer).

Chinese protocol (原文)

入选标准：文献记录的有机溶剂耐受模式菌：P. putida KT2440/S12/DOT-T1E/mt-2、P. taiwanensis VLB120、Rhodococcus erythropolis/jostii/opacus、A. baylyi ADP1、B. cepacia/vietnamiensis、S. maltophilia K279a、Marinobacter、Alcanivorax。双路线合并：路线 P = 15 个耐受株参考蛋白组（63,292 条，作耐受适应背景）；路线 A = UniProt 全文 "organic solvent tolerance" 检索 14,575 条，按名称/功能注释清洗为 3,549 条蛋白级正例（如 LptD）。

文献锚定：株表型 ≠ 蛋白级耐受，因此每个物种带人工评级（严格共现 + 二次回填：high 4 / medium 6 / medium-strain-unverified 3 / weak 或 hydrocarbon-only 3）；Comamonas testosteroni 因缺乏直接溶剂耐受文献被排除出主分析。

实验校准：溶剂效应的实验验证来自 OrganiZymeDB（见实验参数层）。

Mesophilic controls

Inclusion criteria — Four control pools matched by phylogeny and lifestyle: methanogenic archaea for haloarchaea (Methanosarcina ×3, Methanococcus, Methanocorpusculum, Methanospirillum) and mesophilic bacteria for the bacterial pools (E. coli K-12 MG1655, B. subtilis 168, Deinococcus radiodurans, Lactococcus lactis, Corynebacterium glutamicum, M. smegmatis, S. aureus, Shewanella oneidensis and others); organisms shared across pools stored once.

Literature anchoring — No anchoring gate (negative-control background).

Experimental calibration — —

experiments

Inclusion criteria — Four experimental sources aggregated: (i) OrganiZymeDB (27,945 cleaned measurements; 613 thermal-stability records with computable solvent ΔTm entered the experiments layer as 1,839 rows — 613 dTm_solvent + 613 aqueous-Tm + 613 solvent-Tm), linked by UniProt accession; (ii) FireProtDB, 533,071 ΔΔG records, linked by SHA-256 of the wild-type sequence (1,410 matches — the source database is dominated by mesophilic systems; unmatched rows retain the wild-type hash for provenance); (iii) ProTherm-L4, 2,921 single-site ΔTm mutations on 200 PDB structures with Shrake–Rupley RSA; (iv) a literature Tm-versus-pH compilation (1,883 records, 294 linked by SHA-1 prefix).

Literature anchoring — Meltome-scale predicted-Tm datasets were deliberately excluded so the experiments layer contains strictly experimental values.

Experimental calibration — Every record carries source and source_ref (DOI or source record id); verified flags require written evidence.

Chinese protocol (原文)

入选标准：四个实验来源聚合：① OrganiZymeDB 全量导出清洗（27,945 条溶剂测量；其中 613 条热稳定测量进入 EnvTolDB 实验层，展开为 1,839 行 = 613 dTm_solvent + 613 水相 Tm + 613 溶剂相 Tm），按 UniProt accession 直连；② FireProtDB 全库 533,071 条 ddG，按野生型序列 SHA-256 自动比对挂接（1,410 条命中——该库以常温菌为主，未命中行保留 wt_sha256 可回查）；③ ProTherm-L4 2,921 条单点 ΔTm（202 个 PDB 结构，Shrake-Rupley RSA）；④ 文献 Tm-pH 编译 1,883 条（sha1 前缀比对，294 命中）。

文献锚定：meltome 级预测 Tm 数据集被刻意排除，保证实验层严格只含实验测量。

实验校准：每条记录带 source / source_ref（doi 或源记录号）；人工核验 verified 必须写 evidence。

Cold-adapted

Chinese protocol (原文)

入选标准：文献确认的冷适应生物：γ-proteobacteria（Colwellia、Pseudoalteromonas、Psychrobacter、Psychromonas、Moritella、Shewanella 冷栖种）、Bacteroidetes（Psychroflexus、Polaribacter、Psychroserpens、Flavobacterium psychrophilum 等）、Firmicutes/Actinobacteria（Carnobacterium、Cryobacterium、Subtercola、Paenibacillus glacialis）、冷产甲烷古菌（Methanococcoides burtonii、Polaromonas）。分级：psychrophilic（OGT<15°C）/ cold_tolerant（0–20°C 且明确冷适应）。序列口径：光标翻页全量下载（含株级子树），≥50 aa。

文献锚定：Europe PMC 共现检索（物种名 × psychrophilic/psychrotolerant/cold-adapted/cold-active）：9/29（31%）——嗜冷文献常用 cold-adapted 等词在正文而非标题/摘要，保守口径低估为如实记录；后续可用全文检索放宽。

实验校准：BacDive OGT 字段待补抓；温度维度双向检验（thermo +0.006 vs cold 预期负向偏移）

Shared retrieval pipeline

1. UniProt taxonomy API — exact scientific-name resolution → taxon ID
2. Reference proteome where available, else all proteins of the taxon (REST, TSV)
3. Filter ≥50 aa, standard amino acids; MD5 exact dedup
4. Redundancy scope per pool (recorded): thermo = 30% cluster reps;
   salt / pH / solvent = 90% linclust reps; controls = exact dedup
5. Literature anchoring: Europe PMC co-occurrence (species × environment terms, title/abstract)
6. BacDive calibration: Halophily table (NaCl → M) and optimal culture pH
